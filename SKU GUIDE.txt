LONGLEY'S PLUMBING SUPPLIES - ITEM NUMBER (SKU) GUIDE
==========================================

Every item number is a 3-letter category code, a dash, and a 4-digit number:

    PED-0001      PV8-0012      ELT-0007      TLP-0003

The old QuickBooks numbers are no longer used. To look up an old number, open
"Item List Update\sku-crosswalk.csv" (old number -> new number).

NEW ITEMS
  Inventory > New Item. Choose the category first and the POS suggests the next number
  in that category (for example TUB-0001 for your first tub). A brand-new category starts at
  NEW-0001; give it a 3-letter code of your own and keep to it.
